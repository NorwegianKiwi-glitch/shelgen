Home lab

My self-hosted alternative to Big Tech's cloud — run for privacy, cost, control, and the fun of building it myself.

💾

Why I started

I ran out of space on Google Drive for my photos, and didn't want to pay Google to store them, not when Google trains AI models on that data and sells it to advertisers. A homelab let me fix that while getting three things at once: privacy, lower cost, and full control over my own data, plus a standing excuse to keep learning.

🔓

My philosophy

Stay as free from big tech companies as I reasonably can. I self-host whatever I can and feel comfortable running myself, email is one thing I'm leaving to the professionals, and I reach for open-source software whenever it's a real option.

Hardware

Raspberry Pi 4 — 8GB RAM 250GB external SSD Planned: larger SSD

Services

🌐

Webserver

Hosts this very portfolio site, shelgen.no.

☁️

Nextcloud

File storage and sync across all my devices — my actual replacement for Google Drive.

🔑

Vaultwarden

Self-hosted password manager, compatible with the Bitwarden clients.

🖼️

Immich

Stores and organizes my private photos and videos.

🤖

n8n

Automates day-to-day tasks, from a daily news digest to a scheduled flirty good-morning message to my friends, just for fun.

🎵

Navidrome

Self-hosted music streaming for my own library.

🛡️

Sentinal

My self-built SOAR app, running in its own CasaOS container. Scans my other containers for CVEs and gives me a dashboard of traffic hitting my services.

Also tried: Jellyfin (retired, the Pi 4 wasn't powerful enough) and MFA (paused after locking myself out once, too scared to try again just yet).
