Management repair system

A digital repair-ticket system built for Nøsted's winch repairs, replacing a pen-and-paper process with a tracked workflow from intake to completion.

C# .NET ASP.NET MVC Tailwind CSS MariaDB
📝➡️💻

From pen and paper to a tracked digital workflow

Nøsted, a local company, partnered with the University of Agder's bachelor program in IT and Information Systems to digitize their existing pen-and-paper process for repairing winches. Built by a group of bachelor students as a capstone project, the system gives Nøsted's staff a single web app, built with C# and ASP.NET MVC on a MariaDB backend, to log, assign, and track every repair job from intake to completion.

How it works

🔐

Login, roles & accounts

Staff sign in with role-based access; admins can create and delete user accounts as roles change.

🎫

Repair ticket logging

Log a new repair ticket in a couple of clicks instead of a paper form.

📶

Status tracking

Follow a ticket through every stage: assigned, repaired, closed.

🧾

Customer & equipment records

Every ticket links back to a customer and the specific winch being serviced.

🧑‍🔧

Technician assignment

Admins assign tickets to the technician who'll handle the repair.

📊

Reporting

Generate reports across tickets, technicians, and turnaround time.

🛠️

Full CRUD operations

Create, read, update, and delete tickets, customers, and equipment records.

🗄️

MariaDB-backed storage

All records persisted in MariaDB for a reliable audit trail.
